# Backlink kit — arunasvismantas.eu

The goal is **DR growth via diverse, relevant, dofollow links from many domains.**
One link each from ten domains beats fifty from one. Work top-down: the top is
easy and in your control, the bottom is slower but moves DR the most.

**Rules for every link:** dofollow, in-content (not a site-wide footer), anchor =
your name or the URL (never a keyword like "AI consultant"), pointed at the
**root** `https://arunasvismantas.eu`.

Track everything in the table at the bottom so you know what's landed.

## Tier 3 — Editorial & independent (the real DR mover)

### A. Network / accelerator cohort asks (fastest independent links)
Your 500 Global Batch 9, FirstPick, and Latitude59 cohorts + past podcast guests
are warm contacts who link each other. Short DM/email:

> Hey [Name], I just launched my personal site — arunasvismantas.eu — where I write about building with AI. Would you add a link from your team/about/resources page? Happy to return the favour and link you from mine. 2-minute thing.

### B. LT press pitch (biggest single wins: vz.lt, bznstart, 15min/verslas, madeinvilnius, delfi/verslas)

>
> Laba diena, [vardas]. Esu Arunas Vismantas, Callsy founderis. Per pastaruosius metus Callsy pristatėme „Rykliuose. >
> Manau, tai galėtų būti įdomi istorija apie tai, kaip AI keičia įkūrimo žaidimą Lietuvoje. Galiu pasidalinti duomenimis, ekrano vaizdais ir atsakyti į klausimus. Ar būtų įdomu?

### C. Guest podcast pitch (you co-host Socialhack → swap)
> Hey [host], I co-host the Socialhack podcast and build AI voice agents at Callsy. I'd love to come on [show] to talk about how a non-technical founder ships real AI products. Happy to host you on Socialhack too. Here's a recent one I did: [Perception Paradox link].

Then ask each host to add your link in the episode **show notes on their site**
(that's the independent backlink; the YouTube description is nofollow).
